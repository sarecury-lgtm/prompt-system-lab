## Structure summary

Static pre-run rubric: six dimensions, each scored 1-3 with concrete anchors, summed into risk bands and illustrated through worked examples.

## Pattern lesson

An Evaluation Rubric should make prompt quality observable before execution by scoring specific construction features and tying totals to concrete decisions.

## Mechanism

The rubric converts vague review into anchored judgments: each dimension has a named failure mode, score descriptions, and a diagnostic use for revision.

## Failure mode

A prompt may produce a good answer by luck, or a strong prompt may fail due to model fit; the rubric reduces construction risk but does not replace output testing.

## Reusable move

Before running a reusable prompt, score it for task specificity, role, context, format, constraints, and verifiability, then revise the lowest dimensions first.
