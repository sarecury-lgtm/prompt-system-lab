## Evidence note

Observed the Reddit post directly. It supplies paired evaluator and refinement prompts, a 35-criterion rubric, 1-5 scoring, strengths, improvements, rationales, consistency checks, contrarian review, assumptions, total score, and a repeatable refine/re-evaluate loop.

## Short excerpt

The prompt evaluates across 35 criteria, gives each criterion a score, strength, improvement, and rationale, then uses a second prompt to refine and repeat.

## Structure summary

Two-prompt loop: evaluate with a detailed rubric and validation checks; refine using the critique; validate against purpose, tone, clarity, and instructional logic.

## Pattern lesson

An Evaluation Rubric becomes more useful when paired with an explicit improvement loop that converts scores into concrete revisions and then re-runs evaluation.

## Mechanism

The evaluator generates diagnostic feedback per criterion, while the refiner preserves purpose and structure, applies relevant improvements, and checks for drift before returning a revised prompt.

## Failure mode

A large rubric can create false precision or cognitive overload; without calibration, the model may assign inconsistent scores or overfit to checklist compliance.

## Reusable move

Use a two-pass workflow: first score with criterion-level rationale and consistency checks, then revise against the critique and run a final validation checklist.

## Safety / reproduction note

This is community-authored and not benchmarked; treat the 35 criteria as a reusable structure, not validated weights.
