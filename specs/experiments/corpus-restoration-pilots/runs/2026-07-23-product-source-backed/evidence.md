## Source-quality lesson

A URL from an official or known site is not enough. Before comparison, verify:

1. exact product identity and SKU
2. configuration match
3. page-content consistency
4. measurement method
5. date and market
6. whether price is list, promotion, refurbished, used, or imported
7. whether conflicting evidence is resolved or shown explicitly
